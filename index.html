Try it without a microphone

The demo is a student reciting Surah Al-Mulk from memory. They skip a word, restart an ayah, slip on a sound and say a wrong word. With teacher mode on, listen for the stop signal, then the tick when they fix it.

In teacher mode a qari recites the correct word after each stop. Then say the word, or the whole ayah, to carry on. Use headphones if you can, so the mic does not pick up the qari.

The Sabaq Quran model is Tarteel's open model trained on Quran recitation. It runs on your phone, downloads once (about 38 MB), and is used automatically on iPhone and Safari.

Or type or paste Arabic words. They go through the same engine as speech, a few words at a time.
